# Sales Demo Script (~10 minutes)

**Prep (5 min before the call)**: sign in to the demo dashboard on your laptop AND
phone; run Reset demo data; open Demo Controls in one tab, Leads in another.

## 1. The problem (60 seconds)

> "Quick question — when you're on a roof or under a house and your phone rings,
> what happens to that call?"
>
> Let them answer. Then:
>
> "Industry numbers say 20–30% of calls to home-service companies go unanswered,
> and about 80% of those callers won't leave a voicemail — they just call the next
> company on Google. If your average ticket is $400 and you miss five calls a week,
> that's easily a couple thousand dollars a month walking to your competitor."

## 2. What it does (30 seconds)

> "This system watches your phone line. Anyone you answer — nothing changes. Anyone
> you miss gets a text within seconds, from your business name, and it collects the
> job details for you. You get a text with everything: name, problem, ZIP, how
> urgent. You call back when you're off the roof — and you're calling someone who
> already committed to you by answering questions."

## 3. Live demo (5 minutes)

Screen-share the dashboard, Demo Controls tab.

1. **Click "Simulate missed call."**
   > "A homeowner just called you and you couldn't pick up. Watch what they get."
   Open the new lead — show the outreach text. Point out: "It's your company name,
   and it's compliant — there's an opt-out right there."
2. **Click through the quick script** (YES → Sarah Jones → AC not cooling → …).
   After each click, the bot's next question appears.
   > "No AI improvising, no promises about price or arrival time it can't keep —
   > it asks exactly these questions and stops."
3. **Show the notification** (read the qualified-lead summary out loud):
   > "This is the text that hits your phone. Name, number, problem, ZIP, when to
   > call. Fifteen seconds of reading and you decide who to call first."
4. **Show the leads list** — the seeded pipeline.
   > "Every missed call becomes a card. You mark them booked or won, and put the
   > ticket value on it — takes ten seconds."
5. **Show Reports.**
   > "Every Monday you get this by email. And look — recovered revenue is only what
   > YOU entered. I will never show you an inflated made-up ROI number; you'll see
   > exactly what this thing paid for."
6. **The closer — simulate the emergency** ("I smell gas near the furnace"):
   > "One more thing. If someone texts something dangerous — gas smell, CO alarm —
   > the bot stops selling and tells them to get out and call 911, and you get an
   > immediate alert. That's the kind of thing that matters in this trade."

## 4. Setup expectations (60 seconds)

> "Setup on your side is small: we give you a local tracking number that rings your
> existing phone exactly like today. You put that number on your Google listing and
> website. Your current number keeps working. I do the rest — takes about a day."

## 5. The offer (60 seconds)

> "I'm taking two Dallas pilot companies at $99 setup and $99 a month — that's
> roughly one-quarter of ONE recovered service call. In exchange, you give me
> feedback and, if it works, a short case study. If it doesn't recover its cost,
> cancel any month — no contract. Want one of the two slots?"

## Objection quick answers

- **"My texts/CSR handle it"** → "Great — this only acts when nobody answers. It's
  a net, not a replacement."
- **"Is this legal spam?"** → "They called you first; we send one service follow-up
  with opt-out, on a carrier-registered number. No marketing blasts, ever."
- **"What if it says something wrong?"** → "It can't improvise. Fixed questions,
  never quotes price or arrival, hands anything odd to you immediately."
- **"I don't want to change my number"** → "You don't — your number and phones stay.
  You're just adding a tracking number to your listing. Step two later: forward
  only your *missed* calls from your existing number — your number stays primary."
